Software that runs student and medical transportation, from the morning schedule to the invoice.

The Ark Platform builds and operates the dispatch workstation, the driver app, and the family app that transportation providers use every school day.

What we build

The workstation

One web application for the office: scheduling, routing, dispatch, driver and vehicle records, compliance documents, and billing to districts and agencies.

NC Driver

The driver's app for the day's route. Stops in order, navigation, pick-up and drop-off confirmation, vehicle inspections, and the documents each driver must keep current.

NC Parent

The family's app. Today's ride, the vehicle's position on a map while their rider is on board, and a notification at pick-up and at drop-off.

Who we work with

Transportation providers that carry students for school districts and special education programs, and patients for non-emergency medical transportation. Our software runs daily routes across the Denver metro area today.

Contact
